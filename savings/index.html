Multi-attraction Barcelona tourist passes: do they actually save money?

Sometimes — but only if you pack in enough paid attractions. Bundled multi-attraction passes advertise savings of "up to 50%", but that ceiling only applies once you visit five or more paid attractions within the pass's active window.

Passes make more sense if you're genuinely planning to squeeze in 5 or more paid attractions in a short window and won't have time to book tickets separately.
