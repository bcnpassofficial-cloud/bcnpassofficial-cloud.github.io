Disclosure

BCN Pass Official is an independent, privately run guide created to help visitors find the correct, official booking page for Barcelona's most popular attractions. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially partnered with the City of Barcelona, Sagrada Família, Park Güell, FC Barcelona, or any other attraction listed on this site.

Every ticket link on this site points directly to the attraction's own official website. We do not sell tickets ourselves, add any booking fees, or handle any payments — your purchase happens entirely on the attraction's own site, at the attraction's own official price.

How we choose our links

  • We only link to the ticketing page operated directly by the attraction, its foundation, or its official managing body.
  • We never link to resellers, marketplaces, or third-party ticket agencies, even when they claim to be "official."
  • We review and update links periodically, since attractions occasionally change their booking platforms or domains.
  • The Activities category is added to help you find activity options such as padel courts, sports car rentals and boat trips. We do not promote any third party, and inclusion in this list is not an endorsement.
  • We share these links to the best of our knowledge. The final choice is yours — please choose wisely and confirm details directly with the provider before booking.

How this site is funded

Right now, BCN Pass Official is funded only by optional coffee donations from visitors (see the Home page) — no company, no investors. Some links on this site may in future be part of an official affiliate or partner program, meaning we could earn a small commission if you book through them, at no extra cost to you and never at a higher price than booking directly. This never changes which attraction the link goes to — we only ever link to the attraction's own official ticket page, whether or not that link is part of an affiliate program. If and when any affiliate relationship begins, we'll mark those links clearly and update this page.

Support this site

This site is built and maintained by one family in our spare time. If it saved you a headache while planning your trip, a small coffee via Bizum or PayPal (see the Home page) is always appreciated, but never required.
