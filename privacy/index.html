Privacy Policy

What information we collect

BCN Pass Official does not require an account, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or payment details through this site.

  • Feedback: if you email us or use the Feedback form, we use what you share only to read and respond, and never share it with anyone else.
  • Donations: Bizum and PayPal donations happen entirely within their own systems. We never receive or store your card or bank details.
  • Basic site analytics: standard technical logs (browser type, general location, pages visited) may be collected automatically to keep the site running well. This isn't used to personally identify you.
  • Affiliate links (future): if we join an official affiliate program, clicking certain links may set a tracking cookie with that attraction's own booking platform, similar to a standard "referred by" tag. This does not change the price you pay, and we don't receive any personal data from it beyond knowing a booking was made through our link.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing.
  • We don't contact you unless you contact us first.

Links to other websites

Every ticket-booking link takes you to the attraction's own official site, or in the Activities section, to a third-party provider. Once you leave BCN Pass Official, that site's own privacy policy applies — please review it before entering personal or payment details.

Changes to this policy
