Plan your Barcelona trip

Two kinds of itinerary: ready-made plans inside Barcelona for 1 to 5 days — every day different, and no place visited twice in the same plan — and a step-by-step itinerary for each day trip from the city.

Check before you plan. Many museums and attractions — Sagrada Família, Park Güell, Casa Batlló and others — sell timed tickets that can run out, sometimes weeks ahead. Look at each ticket link first, choose your dates around what is available, then follow the plan. Opening hours below are typical and change by season and holiday; many museums close on Mondays.
