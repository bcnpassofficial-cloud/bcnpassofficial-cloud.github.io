Plan your Barcelona trip, day by day

Pick how many days you have. Each plan runs 8am to 9pm with an hour for lunch, groups the closest sights together so you're not zig-zagging across the city, and tells you how to get from one stop to the next. Days 1–5 stay inside Barcelona and its closest hillside neighbourhoods; from day 6 onward we send you out on a day trip.

Ticketed Free entry Shopping Ticketed stops link to official booking — see our full Tickets and Day Trips pages for more options and alternatives.

More info →

Montserrat's basilica and its famous statue of the Virgin — free to visit.

🚇 At the monastery complex.

Open in Google Maps →
10:30

🚇 Trailhead right by the monastery.

Self-service restaurant on site, or bring a picnic for the trail.

14:00
